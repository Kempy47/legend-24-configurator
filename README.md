# Supercraft Legend 24 — configurator

Interactive 3D configurator for a 2004 Supercraft Legend 24 (26 ft LOA,
2.4 m beam, twin Suzuki 115 hp), built in Mauritius.

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, click any part of the boat to open its
colour control. Recolour the hull, canvas, cushions, deck, rails and
engines; add or remove backrests and bolsters; give the backrests a
diamond-quilted centre panel, plain-bordered, with a contrast stitch in any
colour; hang a 610 x 320 mm preparation board off the aft port rod
holder; switch SeaDek on for the floor and the wall tops; put it on the water; and download a PDF of every
angle in the colours you've chosen.

Everything is one self-contained `index.html` — the 3D model is embedded.
